Privacy Policy

Desert Kings · ملوك الصحراء  |  Effective: 2026-09-01  |  services@mxankoda.com

1. Information We Collect

  • Account: sign-in method (Google / Sign in with Apple / guest), user ID.
  • Profile: nickname, avatar, game statistics (level, power, wins).
  • Gameplay: match records, in-game actions, chat messages, leaderboard ranking.

2. How We Use It

  • To provide online multiplayer matchmaking and gameplay.
  • To maintain leaderboards and player progress.
  • To moderate chat and prevent abuse.

3. Data Storage

Account and game data are stored on Google Firebase (Firestore / Authentication). Online match rooms are relayed through our game server.

4. Third Parties

Firebase (Google), and your chosen sign-in provider (Google / Apple).

5. Chat & Moderation

Chat messages may be reviewed for moderation. You can report or block other players in-game.

6. Your Rights & Deletion

You may delete your account and data at any time via the in-app account screen, or by contacting services@mxankoda.com.

7. Children

This app is not directed at children under 13 (or the applicable minimum age in your region).
