Privacy Policy

Astra Guardians · حُرّاس النجوم  |  Last updated: 2026-10-05  |  services@mxankoda.com

1. Information We Collect

  • Account (optional): email address or phone number and a password hash, used for sign-in and cloud save. You can also play as a guest with only a nickname and backup code.
  • Profile: nickname.
  • Gameplay: wave/score records, leaderboard entries, friends list, and clan membership.
  • Device identifier: used for account security and anti-cheat.
  • Purchase records: only an order identifier and product identifier, received from the store platform.

2. Advertising

Astra Guardians contains no advertising. No advertising SDK is integrated, no advertising identifier is collected, and no data is shared with advertising networks.

3. How We Use It

  • Leaderboards upload only a randomly generated nickname and your wave count — no personal information.
  • Cloud save stores your game progress (heroes, upgrades, currencies) on our servers, linked to your account, if you choose to sign in.
  • Friends and clans store your chosen nickname and public in-game identifiers.
  • Prayer-time feature requests prayer times for Makkah from a public API. No personal data is sent in that request.

4. Data Storage

Account and cloud-save data are stored on our secure backend servers. Local game data is stored on your device.

5. Third Parties

  • Google Play Billing (Android) / App Store (iOS) for purchases — we never receive your payment card details.
  • Hosting and database infrastructure providers required to operate the service.
  • A public prayer-times API for the prayer-time feature.

6. Your Rights & Deletion

You may sign out or delete your account and cloud-save data at any time via the in-app account screen, or by contacting services@mxankoda.com. Uninstalling the app deletes local data.

7. Children

This app is not directed at children under 13 (or the applicable minimum age in your region).
